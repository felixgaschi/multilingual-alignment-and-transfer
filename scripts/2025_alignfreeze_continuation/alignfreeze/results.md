# Results of single-layer realignment and single layer freezing

Pursuing the work done in AlignFreeze, we tried two things:

- freezing more layers during realignment: we freeze all layer but one
- freezing less layers during realignment: we freeze only one layer

The results below show that the impact of the choice of layer is minimal. Single-layer freezing may have a negligeable impact on the results, and the improvements brought by full realignment are already so small that single-layer realignment might not improve much w.r.t. simple fine-tuning.
